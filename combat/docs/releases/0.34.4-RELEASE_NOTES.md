# CombatSolver 0.34.4

- 修复手中有凡庸时，倾泻等自动打牌效果仍被错误计入输出和格挡的问题。
- 出牌限制现在正确包含正在打出的牌及重复打牌；凡庸离开手牌后恢复正常，减少路线预测与实际结算不一致导致的暂停。

- Fixed automatic plays from Cascade and similar effects being incorrectly counted toward damage and Block while Normality is in hand.
- The play limit now includes the card currently being played and repeated plays. The restriction ends when Normality leaves the hand, preventing this source of prediction mismatch and auto-play pauses.
