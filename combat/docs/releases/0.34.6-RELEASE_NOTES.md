# CombatSolver 0.34.6

- 修复谋杀可能重复计算抽牌次数、高估伤害，导致计划外重算的问题。
- 修复柔嫩遇到精密计算触发的奇巧自动出牌时，可能重复扣除力量和敏捷，造成攻击、格挡预测偏差的问题。
- 修复巨斧机器人补货后，新机器人的生命值可能与预测不一致，导致计划外重算的问题。
- 优化路线搜索，减少手牌估值和战斗效果计算中的重复工作与临时内存分配，提高并行搜索效率，保持原有搜索预算和路线评判规则。
- 对于拥有至少 16 个逻辑处理器的电脑，默认搜索并行度由 4 提高到 8；手动设置仍然优先。
- 修复战前预测的强制刷新可能复用旧请求，以及请求合并或命中缓存时，关闭后台预测进程和空闲退出时间设置未正确生效的问题。

- Fixed Murder potentially counting card draws twice and overestimating damage, causing unexpected replans.
- Fixed Tender potentially applying Strength and Dexterity losses twice when Calculated Gamble triggers Sly auto-plays, causing incorrect damage and Block predictions.
- Fixed replacement Axebots potentially spawning with different HP from the prediction, causing unexpected replans.
- Optimized route search by reducing repeated hand-value and combat-effect calculations, lowering temporary allocations, and improving parallel execution while preserving search budgets and route-ranking rules.
- Increased the default search parallelism from 4 to 8 on systems with at least 16 logical processors. Manual settings still take precedence.
- Fixed forced pre-combat refreshes reusing an existing request, and fixed worker shutdown and idle-timeout settings not being applied correctly when requests were shared or served from cache.
